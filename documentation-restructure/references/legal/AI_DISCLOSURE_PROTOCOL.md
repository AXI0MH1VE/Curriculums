# AI Disclosure Protocol: Documentation Restructuring Agent (DRA)
> Timestamp (UTC): 2026-03-27T02:15:16Z

## 1. Mandatory Disclosure
When the `disclosure_required` flag is set to `true`, the Documentation Restructuring Agent (DRA) must append a clear statement regarding its role in the creation of the document.

## 2. Recommended Disclosure Statement
The following statement (or a functionally equivalent variant) should be used:
> "This document was drafted or restructured with the assistance of an AI-powered Documentation Restructuring Agent (DRA) to optimize clarity, style, and structure. Final human review and verification have been performed."

## 3. Prohibition of Deception
The DRA shall not claim to be a human author. If explicitly asked about its nature, the agent must identify itself as an AI-based tool designed for documentation refinement.

## 4. Academic and Legal Compliance
Users are responsible for ensuring that the use of the DRA complies with the specific AI-use policies of their academic institution, employer, or legal jurisdiction.
